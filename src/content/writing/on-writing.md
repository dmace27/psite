The last few weeks I've been looking through the Waterloo cs webring for ideas to make my own personal website. What I've noticed is that a lot of Waterloo students have writing sections of their personal website. While I think this is great, as you're reading this on the writing section of my own website, it worries me a little bit. I am sitting here in my room at 10:58pm about 6 weeks before my first classes at Waterloo start. I've been writing essays for the past month or so and have been journaling for about 7 months by now. I personally use writing as an outlet to voice my thoughts that are too jumbled and large to keep in my head for later articulation. Writing has been a coping method for me when I can't handle what is going on inside my own head, especially when it comes to journaling. This practice has been one of the main catalysts for personal growth that I've experienced. The act of clearing and organizing my thoughts helps me come to new conclusions and revelations. I think I am not very unique in the sense where writing is an act of clearing thoughts and used as a tool for growth among these waterloo cs students. This is the thing that worries me. Obviously I can't speak for everyone and say that writing is only a coping mechanism. But, something is making these students do a lot of growing. Waterloo is renowned as a very tough environment with grueling co-op culture and blah blah blah but it is different when you're staring down the barrel of starting first year. I am scared but also excited. Growth is important and necessary but the things that cause growth are usually bad or at the very least uncomfortableness or change.
